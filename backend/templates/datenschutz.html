Dafür halten wir IP-Adresse und E-Mail-Adresse höchstens 15 Minuten im Arbeitsspeicher; gespeichert werden sie nicht. Erfolgreiche Anmeldungen protokollieren wir mit Ihrem pl8list-Nutzernamen, um Fehler und Missbrauch erkennen zu können; diese Protokolle werden fortlaufend überschrieben (höchstens etwa 3 MB). Damit niemand massenhaft Gastkonten anlegt, begrenzen wir auch diese je IP-Adresse; dafür halten wir die IP-Adresse höchstens zwei Stunden im Arbeitsspeicher. Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO.

You can listen without an account: we then keep what you are shown, play, like and follow under a random guest session cookie, and delete it all when you sign out, or within an hour once the session has gone unused for 30 days; signing in with pl8list later moves it to your account. To sign in, your email address and login code are passed to pl8list.com, which sends you the code. We keep your pl8list user id, handle, display name and avatar id, plus what you are shown, play, finish, skip, like or mark "not for me" and which artists you follow, to recommend tracks. If you choose "stay connected" (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, per device), we store it encrypted with a key derived from your session cookie, which only your browser holds, and use it while you use the app to resync every six hours and to add what you like and follow here to your pl8list library. Withdraw at any time under "account" (stop staying connected, sign out, or delete account); we then log the session out and delete it. IP and email address are held in memory for up to 15 minutes to rate-limit sign-ins, and the IP address for up to two hours to rate-limit new guests. You can download all your data and delete your account in the "account" menu at any time. Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the account and recommendations, Art. 6(1)(f) for delivery and abuse protection. You may complain to a data protection authority.
